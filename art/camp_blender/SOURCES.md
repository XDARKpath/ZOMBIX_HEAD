# Camp props

Original Blender models authored for this project. No external vehicle or log asset dependency.

- camp_rover.glb: expedition 4x4, +X forward, metre scale.
- camp_log.glb: seated camp log, radius 0.28 m and length 2.40 m, centred along Z.

Editable sources are in tools/blender_sources at the workspace root. Rebuild scripts: tools/build_camp_rover.py and tools/build_camp_log.py. Keep .blend files outside Godot's resource directory to avoid requiring Blender on player machines.
